> and kermit/zmodem m4

* **Floating point (new, for awk and cawf):** the rosco libc had none.
  * `src/rosco/lib`: `libm.a` (lib/math in C, `fpmisc.c` with frexp and
    modf in C for the ACK `.s` files) and `fpprintf.o` (fphook.c and
    ecvt.c: printf %e/%f/%g and strtod). Both are made with "long double"
    turned into "double", also in a copy of `stdio/loc_incl.h`. gcc's long
    double is the 96-bit format, which the soft-float runtime can't handle.
  * Soft float: the m68000 multilib `libgcc.a`. Its routines take long and
    double arguments (a compare returns its int in d0 as a long), so the
    missing `-mshort` multilib does not matter. `cmds/Makefile`: `FPLIBS` =
    `fpprintf.o` + a group of libm, libc and libgcc (libc's ext_comp.c needs
    frexp/ldexp).
  * libc: `strcasecmp()` from lib/ip (it is in the ACK libc too; cawf).
* **New in `cmds/Makefile`:**
  * awk: the definitions in `obj/awk` lose their `static` (gcc rejects
    extern-then-static).
  * bc: loads `/usr/lib/libmath.b` for `-l`, as on Minix. The sources are
    built with `-Dconst=` (getopt prototype).
  * yap (also `more`): built with `-D__CC68__ -Dconst=`.
  * m4 (`-DEXTENDED`).
  * cron, crontab: `-fcommon` (tab.h defines `crontabs`).
  * kermit (C-Kermit 4E).
  * rz, sz.
  * cawf (also `nroff`, macros in `/usr/lib/cawf`).
  * From commands/simple: at, mail, man, tget.
  * Stack sizes are those of the ACK build, except `at`: 8kw. With 4kw its
    stack ran into its three stdio buffers: "Memory fault" in 11 of 12 runs
    with output on the tty, 0 of 12 at 8kw.
* **Root fs (`sdproto`):**
  * The commands above in `/usr/bin`, and `/bin/egrep`.
  * `/usr/lib/crontab` (`rootfs/crontab`, an example only).
  * The directories `/usr/run`, `/usr/spool/{at,at/past,crontabs,mail}` and
    `/usr/man`.
  * The scripts adduser, makewhatis, whatis/apropos and clear.
* **Old Minix sh:** whatis.sh and clear.sh were written for ash. The rosco
  copies are in `src/rosco/rootfs/scripts`:
  * clear: plain `PATH=` (sh rejects `export PATH=...`);
  * whatis: no `continue` in nested `for` (sh loops forever: "out of
    string space"), and an egrep pattern in single quotes (sh keeps `\\(`
    inside double quotes).
* **cron** is started by `/etc/rc` (`/usr/bin/cron ... &`). It runs
  `/usr/lib/crontab`, the user crontabs and the at jobs. Job output is sent
  with `mail`.
* **Checked in MAME** (firmware 2.42, 5MB, card at 115200):
  * awk: arithmetic, sqrt/exp, printf `%5.2f %e %g`, -F, string functions;
  * bc -l: `4*a(1)` to 20 digits, `2^100`;
  * m4: define and eval;
  * yap: pages, q quits;
  * nroff -man, man, makewhatis, whatis, apropos, clear;
  * adduser (cpdir /usr/ast, passwd entry);
  * mail to root;
  * rz/sz usage, kermit interactive;
  * at: 12/12 runs;
  * an at job at 23:59 was run by cron, and its output was mailed to root;
  * `crontab -c` with `* * * * *` ran every minute.
* **Not done:**
  * no manual pages in the tree (`/usr/man` is empty);
  * the test suite was not re-run (the only libc change is the added
    strcasecmp).
